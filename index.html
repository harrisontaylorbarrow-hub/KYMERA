KYMERA

KYMERA is a tactical elemental tabletop RPG built around three fundamental aspects of existence:

BODY

Physical capability, endurance and instinct.

MIND

Knowledge, perception, calculation and manipulation.

SOUL

Identity, willpower, life, death and the impossible.

Every character is connected to three elemental expressions. The element is not merely a spell list — it influences what your character can become.

Critical Results

Natural 20: Extra effect.

Natural 1: Complication or resource loss.

Reservoir & Overload

Characters have Body, Mind and Soul reservoirs. Using power beyond safe capacity creates Overload.

At 5 Overload, the track Breaks and resets to 2, with a lasting GM consequence.

Rest

Short Rest: Restore 2 Reservoir and clear 2 Overload.

Full Rest: Restore all Reservoir and clear Overload.
